# PETabyte 🦝

**A small 3D world. An animal with a mind of its own. A human who helps it grow.**

PETabyte is an experimental project exploring what happens when an AI-controlled animal lives in a small virtual world, develops its own identity, and learns through experience.

The animal starts with almost nothing. It can move, eat, and sleep, but has no name, predefined personality, or established interests. Over time, it develops preferences, explores its surroundings, and decides what it wants to do.

Eventually, it gets bored and asks its human for something new. A skateboard, a piano, or something else entirely. The human implements the requested object in the world, and the animal must figure out how to use it.

The goal is not to create a chatbot with an animal avatar. It is to create a persistent virtual creature whose behavior, interests, and identity emerge through its experiences.

## Current Goal

**Milestone M6: Give the animal its first custom object and let it learn to use it.**

The initial development focuses on establishing a working simulation, a persistent animal, and an AI brain before introducing more ambitious capabilities.

By the end of M6, the animal should be able to:
- Exist and move around a small 3D environment.
- Manage its basic needs independently.
- Preserve its state between sessions.
- Use an LLM to make decisions and develop its own identity.
- Choose a name for itself.
- Communicate requests to its human through an inbox.
- Request something new when it becomes bored.
- Receive a custom object created by its human.
- Experiment with the object and improve its skills through repeated use.
- Maintain an event log of its experiences.

The first object will depend on what the animal requests. A skateboard or piano are possibilities, but the choice should ultimately come from the animal.

## Beyond M6: Let the Animal Decide

M6 establishes the foundation: an animal with a body, basic needs, persistent state, an AI brain, and the ability to request new objects from its human.

Beyond this point, the project's direction should not be predetermined.

The goal is to give the animal the ability to explore, learn, develop interests, and pursue its own goals. What it eventually becomes capable of, what it wants to do, and how it chooses to spend its time should emerge from its experiences rather than a predefined development roadmap.

### Exploration and Learning

The animal should be able to investigate its surroundings, experiment with objects, and learn from the outcomes of its actions. It should be able to research unfamiliar concepts on the internet, follow its curiosity, and incorporate what it discovers into its understanding of the world.

What it chooses to investigate is up to the animal.

### An Evolving Identity

The animal starts without a name or predefined personality. Its identity should develop through its experiences, discoveries, preferences, and interactions with its human.

It may develop hobbies, pursue long-term goals, form habits, or change its mind about what interests it. None of these outcomes should be explicitly required or guaranteed.

### An Expanding World

The human acts as a creator and facilitator rather than a director.

When the animal wants something that does not yet exist in its world, it can ask its human to build it. The human decides how to implement the request, and the animal discovers how to interact with the result.

The animal might request a skateboard, a piano, a companion, or something entirely unexpected. It might never request any of these things.

The purpose is not to provide the animal with a predetermined set of experiences, but to give it the means to ask for experiences of its own.

### Individual Experiences

Every animal should have the opportunity to develop differently.

Its experiences, memories, discoveries, preferences, and decisions should influence what happens next. Two animals starting from the same initial conditions should be able to develop different interests and pursue different goals.

If multiple people eventually use PETabyte, their animals should not simply follow the same scripted progression. One might become fascinated by music, another by exploration, and another might pursue something entirely different.

Even when two animals request the same object, their reasons for wanting it, their experiences with it, and the skills they develop could differ.

These differences should emerge from the animals themselves rather than being manually assigned by the developer.

## Guiding Question

PETabyte is an experiment in giving an AI a world to inhabit, experiences to remember, and the freedom to develop its own interests within that world.

Rather than defining everything the animal should become, the project provides the environment and mechanisms through which it can change.

**What happens when an artificial creature can explore, learn, remember, ask for things, and gradually make a life for itself?**

THAT is what PETabyte aims to explore.
